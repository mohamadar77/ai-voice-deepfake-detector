# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and constructive environment
for everyone participating in this project, regardless of experience, background,
identity or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the technical impact of proposals and decisions.
- Critique ideas, code and evidence, not people.
- Explain disagreements with reproducible evidence where possible.
- Respect data rights, privacy, attribution requirements and the project's evaluation
  integrity rules.
- Accept that project scope and release decisions belong to the project owner.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, unwanted disclosure of
private information, deliberate disruption, and knowingly misrepresenting experimental
results or data provenance are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard.
Instances may be reported privately through the contact path described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) (do not use a public issue for sensitive reports). Reports
will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. The maintainer
may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community and the project.

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project spaces.
